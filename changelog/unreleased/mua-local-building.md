A Local World builds: its Account is a Builder with the default limits, and its Worker takes the server's `place`, `edit`, `store`, `destroy` and `stop_editing` messages, tells the client what it has open with `compose` and `closed`, and gives a `snapshot` for Try it. Saves made before this build after loading. Its output lines carry their Suggestions, so placing offers "edit the satchel" offline too.
