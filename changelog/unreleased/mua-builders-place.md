Builders can place a thing they composed in the room they stand in, with the Channel's `place`: an Admin makes an Account a Builder at the `;` prompt, and the World refuses an Account that isn't one, one over its quota, and a composition with anything but `described`, `noun`, `item`, `container` and `readable`. Each placed thing records the Account that made it. Scripts and the admin prompt can now `remove` a component.
